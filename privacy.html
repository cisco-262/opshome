Overview

OpsHome NOC is a monitoring app for websites, ports, SSL certificates, DNS records, and domain expiry. We process only the data needed to provide monitoring, alerts, subscription entitlement, account security, and support.

Information We Process

  • Account data: Sign in with Apple identifier, email address if Apple provides it, account ID, and API credentials used by the app.
  • Device and notification data: device identifier, app version, preferred language, and APNs token when push notifications are enabled.
  • Monitoring data: monitor names, targets, domains, IP addresses, ports, paths, DNS record types, check settings, check results, alert state, and analysis results.
  • Subscription data: App Store product IDs, transaction identifiers, plan status, and expiration status. We do not receive or store your payment card information.
  • Security data: IP address and approximate country, region, or city derived from IP address for security, abuse prevention, and operational statistics.

How We Use Data

We use data to create and secure your account, run monitoring checks, send alerts and expiry reminders according to your plan, show analysis reports, verify Pro entitlement, troubleshoot service issues, and maintain service reliability.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not build advertising profiles. Data may be processed by Apple services including Sign in with Apple, App Store In-App Purchase, and APNs, and by hosting or database providers that operate the service infrastructure.

Retention

Account and monitoring data are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Event history and analysis records are retained according to plan limits and operational limits. If you delete your account, service data associated with the account is scheduled for deletion, subject to legal, security, and backup retention needs.

Your Choices

You can disable push notifications in iOS settings, manage monitors in the app, and delete your account from the account management screen. App Store subscription cancellation is managed through Apple.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect service data, including authenticated API access and secure storage of app credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

OpsHome NOC is not directed to children and is not intended for users under the age required by local law to use online services without parental consent.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact us at opshome@outlook.com.
