2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your data only for what is necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account management and authentication
  • Executing monitoring checks and delivering alerts
  • Ensuring service stability and security auditing

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Service.

3. THIRD-PARTY SHARING

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. The only exception is when required by applicable law or a lawful order from a judicial or regulatory authority.

If we ever introduce third-party data sharing in the future, we will update this policy and notify you in advance.

4. DATA STORAGE & TRANSFER

Your data is stored on our cloud servers. Servers may be located outside your region. We apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, and process it only to the extent necessary to provide the Service.

5. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Service:

  • Active account data is retained while your account exists
  • Free plan event history is retained for 7 days; Pro plan event history is retained for up to 90 days
  • Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed
  • Disabled monitor history is deleted 7 days after Pro plan expiry

6. DATA EXPORT

The current version does not provide data export functionality. Please keep your own records of important configurations.

7. SECURITY

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. API keys and push tokens are stored in iOS Keychain and never written to UserDefaults. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access and correct your account information within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data via in-app settings
  • Withdraw push notification consent via iOS system settings
  • Revoke OpsHome NOC™ access to your Apple ID via Settings → Apple ID → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy at any time. Updates will be announced via the app or App Store page. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact: opshome@outlook.com
