Plans and Subscriptions

Downgrade or Expiration

If Pro expires or is cancelled, your account returns to Free entitlement from the expiration time. Monitor settings are retained, but monitors above the Free limit may be placed in a disabled state. Check history, event records, and analysis records then follow the Free 7-day rolling retention period. History for disabled over-limit monitors may be kept for 7 days after expiration and then deleted.

Before Pro expires, the app may show a reminder once per day on first login during the final 7 days. The app may also show a notice when the account is downgraded and another notice when the over-limit history cleanup is completed after the grace period.

Alerts, reminders, and analysis are generated from network checks and system rules. They may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or inaccurate due to network conditions, third-party services, device settings, Apple push delivery, or other operational factors.
