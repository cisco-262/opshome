Overview

OpsHome NOC is a monitoring app for websites, ports, SSL certificates, DNS records, and domain expiry. We process only the data needed to provide monitoring, alerts, subscription entitlement, account security, and support.

Information We Process

  • Account data: information needed to sign in, identify your account, and provide secure access to the service.
  • App and notification data: necessary app and device-related information used to deliver the service, language display, and push notifications.
  • Limited product usage records: such as feature visits, used to improve product experience, troubleshoot issues, and maintain service reliability. We do not use this data for advertising profiling or sell personal data.
  • Monitoring data: monitor configuration, user-provided targets, check results, alert status, and analysis results.
  • Subscription data: App Store product IDs, transaction identifiers, plan status, and expiration status. We do not receive or store your payment card information.
  • Basic service records may include country-level access information for security and operational statistics.

Monitoring Targets, Credentials, and Integrations

You may provide monitor targets such as domains, URLs, IP addresses, ports, Docker Probe names, status page settings, Telegram chat identifiers, Telegram bot tokens, and future infrastructure integration settings such as Proxmox API endpoints or tokens.

Credentials and tokens you provide are used only to deliver the feature you configure, such as sending Telegram alerts or reading authorized infrastructure state. Sensitive integration secrets are stored using encryption controls where supported by the service and are not shown publicly.

Docker Probe and Private Network Data

Docker Probe runs in your private network and connects outbound to OpsHome Cloud. OpsHome NOC does not require you to open inbound ports and does not proxy your business traffic.

For private monitoring, we process the minimum operational data needed to provide the service, such as probe heartbeat, assigned monitor tasks, check results, latency, status, error summaries, and limited host or asset metadata. We do not collect the content of your private services.

How We Use Data

We use data to create and secure your account, run monitoring checks, send alerts and expiry reminders according to your plan, show analysis reports, verify Pro entitlement, troubleshoot service issues, and maintain service reliability.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not build advertising profiles. Data may be processed by Apple services used for sign-in, subscriptions, and notifications, and by hosting or database providers that operate the service infrastructure.

Some optional features depend on third-party services, such as Telegram for alert delivery, DNS/WHOIS/RDAP providers for domain-related checks, and infrastructure providers used to operate and secure the service. When you click a third-party link or configure a third-party integration, that provider's own terms and privacy policy may apply.

Retention

Account and monitor settings are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Check history, event records, and analysis records are kept on a rolling basis: Free keeps up to 7 days and Pro keeps up to 90 days. If you delete a monitor, the history for that monitor is deleted immediately.

If Pro expires, entitlement changes to Free from the expiration time. Monitor history then follows the Free 7-day rolling retention period. Monitor settings are kept, but monitors above the Free limit may be disabled. History for disabled over-limit monitors may be kept for a 7-day grace period after Pro expiration and then deleted.

You may delete your OpsHome NOC account from the account management screen in the app. Account deletion removes your OpsHome account access and deletes service data such as monitor settings, monitor history, analysis records, notification records, registered devices, and push tokens, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, dispute, and backup retention needs.

Account deletion does not delete your Apple ID and does not cancel any App Store subscription. You are responsible for managing and cancelling subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings. If you sign in again with Apple after deleting your OpsHome account, a new empty OpsHome account may be created.

Your Choices

You can disable push notifications in iOS settings, manage monitors in the app, delete your OpsHome NOC account from the account management screen, and manage App Store subscription cancellation through Apple.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect service data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

OpsHome NOC is not directed to children and is not intended for users under the age required by local law to use online services without parental consent.

Your Rights (EEA, UK & Brazil)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Brazil, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@opshome.run. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data. You can also delete your account directly in the app.
  • Restriction: Ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format by contacting us.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. EU residents can find their authority at edpb.europa.eu.

Public Status Pages

OpsHome NOC includes an optional public status page feature. If you create a status page, you choose which monitors appear on it and share the link. The page is publicly accessible while enabled.

Public status pages show only: the display names you assign to monitors, monitor type, current status, uptime summary, and the type and time of recent events.

Public status pages never reveal: real IP addresses, hostnames, URLs, Docker container names, internal network paths, API keys, probe identifiers, or any other sensitive target details.

You can disable or delete a status page at any time from within the app. Disabling makes the page immediately inaccessible to the public.

For privacy requests, contact us at support@opshome.run.
