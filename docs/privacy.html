Overview

OpsHome NOC is a monitoring app for websites, ports, SSL certificates, DNS records, and domain expiry. We process only the data needed to provide monitoring, alerts, subscription entitlement, account security, and support.

Information We Process

  • Account data: information needed to sign in, identify your account, and provide secure access to the service.
  • App and notification data: necessary app and device-related information used to deliver the service, language display, and push notifications.
  • Monitoring data: monitor configuration, user-provided targets, check results, alert status, and analysis results.
  • Subscription data: App Store product IDs, transaction identifiers, plan status, and expiration status. We do not receive or store your payment card information.
  • Basic service records may include country-level access information for security and operational statistics.

How We Use Data

We use data to create and secure your account, run monitoring checks, send alerts and expiry reminders according to your plan, show analysis reports, verify Pro entitlement, troubleshoot service issues, and maintain service reliability.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not build advertising profiles. Data may be processed by Apple services used for sign-in, subscriptions, and notifications, and by hosting or database providers that operate the service infrastructure.

Retention

Account and monitor settings are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Check history, event records, and analysis records are kept on a rolling basis: Free keeps up to 7 days and Pro keeps up to 90 days. If you delete a monitor, the history for that monitor is deleted immediately.

If Pro expires, entitlement changes to Free from the expiration time. Monitor history then follows the Free 7-day rolling retention period. Monitor settings are kept, but monitors above the Free limit may be disabled. History for disabled over-limit monitors may be kept for a 7-day grace period after Pro expiration and then deleted. You may clear OpsHome NOC service data from the account management screen, subject to legal, security, and backup retention needs.

Your Choices

You can disable push notifications in iOS settings, manage monitors in the app, clear OpsHome NOC service data from the account management screen, and manage App Store subscription cancellation through Apple.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect service data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

OpsHome NOC is not directed to children and is not intended for users under the age required by local law to use online services without parental consent.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact us at opshome@outlook.com.
