// FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about security, data transmission, plans, and supported infrastructure.

What is the main difference between Free and Pro?
Regular Free is designed for public monitoring and includes up to 30 public monitor targets. Pro raises the public limit and adds Docker Probe private monitoring, private infrastructure integrations, up to 5 Docker Probe devices and 50 private monitor targets.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription at any time?
Yes. Cancel through your Apple ID subscription settings at any time. Pro remains available until the end of the current paid billing period. Deleting the app or your OpsHome account does not cancel the App Store subscription.
Is Docker Probe difficult to install?
Follow the documentation and run the provided Docker deployment commands. Users familiar with basic Docker operations can usually complete installation in a few minutes. View deployment guide and video
Is my data secure, and where is it stored?
Supported monitoring status and infrastructure metrics are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS and processed by hosted OpsHome Cloud infrastructure and database providers. OpsHome does not require uploading NAS files, documents, photos, or payment card information.
Why is the final subscription price confirmed in the App Store?
Apple shows the final local currency, taxes, eligible offers, trials, and billing confirmation for your App Store region before purchase. OpsHome does not receive or store your payment card information.
Can I use OpsHome on multiple devices or with Family Sharing?
You can sign in on another supported device with the same Sign in with Apple account and restore an active Pro purchase if needed. Family Sharing is not currently included in the published OpsHome plan entitlement.
Is Docker Probe secure?
Docker Probe initiates an outbound connection to OpsHome Cloud. It does not require inbound port forwarding or exposing private services directly to the internet.
How is monitoring data transmitted?
Docker Probe collects supported service status and infrastructure metrics, then sends them to the OpsHome Cloud API through an encrypted HTTPS connection.
Does monitoring continue when the iPhone app is closed?
Yes. Checks continue through OpsHome Cloud or Docker Probe. The iPhone app is used to review status, reports, and notifications.
