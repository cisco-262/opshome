Synology NAS Monitoring for Your Homelab

Your NAS is usually one of the most important boxes in a homelab. OpsHome NOC helps you review supported Synology NAS health, volumes, packages, temperatures, Docker Probes, Asset Sources, and Private Monitors from your iPhone.

See NAS health without exposing DSM

A Synology NAS often hosts storage, Docker apps, backups, photos, media services, and internal admin pages. It should not need a public DSM panel just so you can see whether it is healthy.

OpsHome works with Docker Probe and authorized Asset Sources to surface supported NAS status in a mobile NOC view. You can use OpsHome for quick review and alerts while keeping Synology DSM for administration and deeper troubleshooting.

What OpsHome can show for Synology

NAS health

Review supported overall health signals so the NAS is visible beside public services and Private Monitors.

Volumes and storage

Keep storage context close to the services that depend on it instead of checking several screens for a first look.

Packages and services

See supported package and service context when available from the configured Asset Source.

Temperatures and hardware

Surface supported temperature and hardware signals that may indicate the NAS needs attention.

Private services stay private

Many Synology deployments include internal dashboards, Docker apps, backup panels, and management surfaces. OpsHome is designed to monitor the state of private services without asking you to expose those interfaces publicly.

Docker Probe runs inside your network and reports supported status outbound to OpsHome Cloud. As with every Asset Source or Private Monitor, only configure systems you own or are authorized to monitor.

FAQ

Does Synology DSM need to be exposed to the internet?
No. Use Docker Probe inside your private network instead of exposing the Synology management interface publicly.
Does OpsHome replace Synology DSM?
No. Use DSM for administration. Use OpsHome for quick status review, alerts, and NOC-style visibility.
What Synology signals can OpsHome show?
OpsHome can show supported NAS health, volumes, packages, temperatures, and private service context when configured through an authorized Asset Source.
Does OpsHome need my NAS files?
No. OpsHome monitoring does not require NAS files, photos, documents, or payment card information.
