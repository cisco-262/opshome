OpsHome NOC Support Center

Get help, report issues, and share feedback directly with the OpsHome team.

OpsHome NOC includes an integrated support and feedback system designed for self-hosted infrastructure monitoring.

In-App Support

The fastest way to contact the OpsHome team is through the built-in feedback system.

Open OpsHome NOC -> Me -> Help & Support -> Feedback Center

Submit questions, report problems, request features, or ask for assistance without leaving the app.

Feedback Center in OpsHome NOC

Submit questions, report issues, request features, and communicate with the OpsHome team directly from the app.

Feedback Center allows users to submit and track support requests directly inside OpsHome NOC.

Feedback Categories

  • General - Questions, suggestions, and general feedback.
  • Bug - Report unexpected behavior or technical issues.
  • Feature - Request improvements or new capabilities.
  • Billing - Subscription and purchase related questions.
  • Alert - Monitoring notifications and alert behavior.
  • Asset - Infrastructure assets and monitored resources.
  • Account - Account and data management questions.

Track Your Requests

Each feedback submission is tracked through the OpsHome support workflow.

  • Open - Your request has been received and is waiting for review.
  • Closed - The request has been reviewed or resolved.

Previous submissions remain available through Submission History.

Privacy-Focused Support

Only information that you provide in the feedback form is submitted with your account.

  • No automatic device information collection
  • No automatic system log collection
  • No automatic screenshot collection
  • No access to personal files
